Decks, garages and outbuildings take a beating. They carry snow and rain, cars and workbenches, and people leaning on railings. The structure underneath has to be built properly, even when nobody will see it once the job is finished.

For decks, the important work is the part that holds everything up. We frame posts, beams and joists to your drawings, attach ledgers to the house with the flashing details your plans call for, and build stairs and landings that feel solid underfoot. A deck that bounces or pulls away from the house is a framing problem, and it's one we don't leave behind.

Garages are small buildings with real structural demands. Wide door openings need properly sized headers. Walls need to be braced against wind and racking. Roofs need to handle our rain and the occasional heavy snow. If you're putting living space above the garage, the floor system and fire separation need extra attention. We frame to your approved drawings and check the details that matter before we build.

We also frame workshops, sheds, carports and covered patios. Whatever the building, we start with a square, level layout so doors close properly, roofing goes on cleanly and everything lines up.

Many of these jobs happen on a lot where people already live, so we keep the work tidy. We stack material neatly, clean up offcuts and nails every day and keep your driveway and yard as usable as we can.

When the framing is done, it's ready for inspection, roofing and the trades that finish the job.
