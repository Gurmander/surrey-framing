Multi-family framing is a different job from a single house. The same wall gets built ten or twenty times, the schedule is tighter, and a mistake in one unit can repeat right down the row. It takes a crew that's organized, consistent and easy to work with.

With BC's small-scale multi-unit housing rules, more lots in Surrey and across the Lower Mainland are getting townhouses, rowhouses and multiplexes. We frame these projects for general contractors and developers who need a sub they can count on.

Before we start, we go through the architectural and structural drawings with your team. We look at party walls, shear walls, hold-downs, beam schedules and stair layouts, and we raise questions early so answers come back before the crew needs them. On site, we lay out carefully so units stay consistent and the other trades find what they expect in every unit.

We frame floor systems, exterior and party walls, roofs, stairs, landings and balconies, then sheathe and add blocking and backing. We follow the engineer's nailing and hold-down schedules, because those details are exactly what the inspector checks.

Coordination matters as much as carpentry. We work through your site super, keep our area of the site tidy and plan our sequence so plumbing, electrical and mechanical can follow without waiting on us. If a delivery is late or the weather turns, you hear about it from us first, along with a plan.

When a building is framed, we walk it unit by unit, deal with any deficiencies and hand it over ready for inspection. Safety documents and proof of insurance are available on request before we start.
