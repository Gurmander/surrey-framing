A laneway home or garden suite is small, but it isn't simple. You're fitting a full house onto a corner of an existing lot, often with tight lane access, overhead wires, neighbours close by and a family still living in the main house. The framing has to be just as precise as a full-size home, in a fraction of the space.

We plan the job before the first delivery. Where will the lumber go? How will trusses get in? Where can the crew work without blocking the lane or your driveway? Sorting this out early keeps the site safe and keeps your neighbours on side.

Small homes often have more detail per square foot than big ones. Lofts, dormers, vaulted ceilings, large windows and compact stairs all need careful framing so they work and pass inspection. We frame to your architectural and structural drawings, and if something doesn't add up on paper, we ask before we build.

We frame the floor or set out walls on your slab, build exterior and interior walls, frame the roof or set trusses, and sheathe everything. We add blocking for kitchens and bathrooms so cabinets, vanities and grab bars have something solid to fasten to.

Whether you're building a suite for family, a rental for extra income or a place to downsize into, we treat your yard with respect. Offcuts get cleaned up daily, materials are stacked neatly and your main house stays accessible.

When the frame is up, we walk it with you, fix anything that needs attention and leave it ready for the framing inspection.
