A new home is only as good as its frame. If the walls aren't plumb and the floors aren't flat, every trade after us pays for it: drywall cracks, cabinets don't sit right and doors stick. We frame it right the first time so the rest of the build goes smoothly.

We start with your architectural and structural drawings. Before the first plate goes down, we go through the plans, check beam sizes and point loads, and raise questions early. Questions on paper are cheap. Fixing a wall after it's sheathed is not.

On site, we build from the sill plate up: floor systems, walls, beams and posts, stairs, roof framing and sheathing. We set trusses, install engineered joists and add the blocking and backing your other trades will need later, so nobody has to cut into a finished wall to hang a cabinet or a grab bar.

We keep the site clean and organized. Offcuts get stacked, nails get picked up and the lot stays safe for your family, your neighbours and the next crew in. When the frame is done, we walk it with you or your builder, fix anything that's off and leave it ready for the framing inspection.

Building your own home for the first time? We explain what happens at each stage, what you need to have ready and when the inspector needs to see the work. If you're a small builder, we fit into your schedule and work alongside your site super.

Whether it's a modest single-storey house or a large custom home with a complex roof, you get the same care: square, plumb and built to the drawings.
