A finished basement adds living space without adding to your footprint. It can become a family room, extra bedrooms or a secondary suite that helps with the mortgage. All of that starts with framing that's straight, square and laid out exactly where your plans say.

Basements come with their own challenges. Concrete walls are rarely perfectly straight, floors slope toward drains, and ducts, pipes and beams run wherever they had to. We frame around all of it. Perimeter walls are built straight even when the concrete isn't, bulkheads are framed neatly around ducts and plumbing, and partition walls go exactly where your layout needs them.

Moisture is the other big issue below grade. We use pressure-treated plates where wood meets concrete and follow the insulation and vapour barrier details on your drawings, so the walls can be insulated and finished properly.

If you're building a secondary suite, the details matter even more. Fire separations, egress windows, ceiling heights and separate entrances are set by the City of Surrey and your designer. We frame to your approved drawings so the suite is ready for inspection.

We also think about the trades that follow us. We add blocking for kitchen and bathroom cabinets, vanities, handrails and wall-mounted TVs, and we leave clear paths for electrical and plumbing runs.

Basement work happens inside your home, so we keep it tidy. We protect stairs and finished floors on the way in, keep offcuts contained and clean up at the end of each day. When we're done, the space is ready for insulation, drywall and the framing inspection.
