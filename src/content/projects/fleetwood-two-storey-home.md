A two-storey, four-bedroom home on a standard Fleetwood lot. The plans called for an open main floor with an engineered beam carrying the second floor, a covered front entry and a hip roof on trusses.

We framed the floor systems, walls and stairs, set and braced the trusses, and sheathed the walls and roof before the fall rain arrived. Blocking for the kitchen, bathrooms and stair rails went in before the inspection, so the trades that followed had solid backing everywhere they needed it.
