A compact laneway home at the back of an existing lot, with a sleeping loft, a shed dormer and large south-facing windows.

Access was from the lane only, so we planned lumber and truss deliveries around the neighbours and kept the lane clear every evening. The loft floor and dormer were framed to the engineer's details, and the owners stayed in the main house throughout.
