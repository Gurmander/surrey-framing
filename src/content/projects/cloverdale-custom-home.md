A large custom home on an acreage lot, with a vaulted great room, a covered rear patio and a mix of trusses and stick-framed roof sections.

The vault and the ridge beam were stick-framed on site, while the rest of the roof used trusses. We reviewed the architectural and truss drawings together before starting, which caught a bearing conflict before the crane arrived.
