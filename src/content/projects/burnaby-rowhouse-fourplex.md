A side-by-side fourplex on a single residential lot, built under BC's small-scale multi-unit housing rules for a small developer.

Each unit had three levels, with party walls between units and a shared roof. We coordinated closely with the developer's site lead so deliveries, inspections and the other trades stayed on schedule.
