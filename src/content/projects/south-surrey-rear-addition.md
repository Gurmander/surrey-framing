A single-storey addition to extend the kitchen of a 1980s home. The existing rear wall came out and a new engineered beam opened the old kitchen into the new space.

We shored the floor above, set the beam and posts, then framed the addition's floor, walls and roof, matching the existing floor height and tying the new roof into the old one.
