The owners wanted to open their kitchen, dining and living rooms into one space. The wall between them was load-bearing.

We installed temporary shoring, removed the wall, and set a new engineered beam and posts to the structural drawings. New openings were reframed and blocking added for the kitchen before the drywallers arrived.
