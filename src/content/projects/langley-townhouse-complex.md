A row of six three-storey townhouses for a general contractor in Willoughby. Each unit had its own garage, a rooftop deck and party walls with fire separations between units.

We worked unit by unit so plumbing and electrical could follow close behind. Shear walls and hold-downs were installed to the engineer's schedule, and we walked each unit with the site super before calling for inspection.
