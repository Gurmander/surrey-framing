We're looking for an experienced framer to join our crew. You'll lay out and frame floors, walls and roofs on new homes, laneway houses and multi-family projects, working from engineered drawings.

You know how to read a set of plans, you take pride in square and plumb work, and you look out for the people working around you. In return you get steady work, a crew that plans ahead and a safe site.
